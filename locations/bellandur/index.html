Where Bellandur sits

Bellandur sits on the Outer Ring Road (ORR) in South-East Bengaluru. It is a mature job hub. Ecoworld, Embassy Tech Village, Cessna Business Park and RMZ Ecospace are all close by. The ORR tech parks keep demand for homes high.

Adarsh has one live project here, Adarsh Rosewood.

Adarsh Rosewood in Bellandur

Adarsh Rosewood is a small, low-rise project of 2 and 3 BHK flats. The brochure lists 118 units on G + 4 floors. The sales lounge is on Doddakannelli Road, in Phase 3 of Adarsh Palm Retreat.

Commute and metro

Adarsh's brochure lists these approximate distances from Rosewood:

Adarsh's website places Rosewood next to RMZ Ecospace, but its brochure says 3 km. Map the route from the gate to your office.

Bellandur has a station on the ORR Phase 2A metro line, from Silk Board to KR Puram. Deccan Herald reported in August 2026 that trials start in October and the line may open by March 2027. Prestige Group bought 30-year naming rights to the station in February 2026, per Deccan Herald. Adarsh gives no walking distance from Rosewood.

Short distances do not mean short drives. Deccan Herald reported that ORR white-topping should run from October 2026 to January 2027. Doddakannelli Road feeds the ORR and Sarjapur Road. Test the route at 9 am and 6 pm.

Who Bellandur suits

A good fit

  • ORR tech-park staff who want a quiet, low-rise home a short trip from work.
  • Buyers who want a 2 BHK. Among the Adarsh launches we cover, only Rosewood offers one.
  • Buyers who can pay a Bellandur price plus about 12.6% in taxes and fees.

A poor fit

  • Buyers who want a big campus with a large club.
  • People who commute daily to Whitefield or Electronic City.
  • Buyers who need a metro stop within walking distance soon.

Want a bigger campus nearby? See Adarsh Parkland Phase 2 in Panathur or Adarsh Tropica Phase 2 off Sarjapur Road.

Checks before you book

Read the completion date on the K-RERA portal, not the brochure. Ask for the title deed, the khata and the encumbrance record. Our A-khata vs B-khata vs E-khata guide explains why the khata matters for resale and loans.

Get a loan sanction before you book. Our plot loan vs home loan guide explains how lenders fund a flat. To weigh a flat against land, read plot vs apartment in Bangalore.
