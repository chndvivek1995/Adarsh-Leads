Where Devanahalli sits

Devanahalli is a taluk in Bengaluru Rural district. It sits next to Kempegowda International Airport (KIAL), on the north side of the city.

Buyers come for the airport jobs, new roads and a metro line under work. None of these is a promise of profit, and some timelines have slipped.

Roads, metro and the airport

  • Airport: KIAL handled 44.47 million passengers in FY 2025-26, per Deccan Herald (May 2026).
  • NH 44: Also called Bellary Road, it links the airport side to Hebbal and the city.
  • STRR: The Satellite Town Ring Road's Doddaballapur–Hoskote section has carried tolled traffic since November 2023, per Deccan Herald.
  • Metro: BMRCL is building the Hebbal–airport section. Deccan Herald reported in August 2026 that it is unlikely to open before September 2027.
  • Near the layout: Adarsh puts Stonehill International School and the Padukone-Dravid Centre about 5 minutes away.

The airport metro ends at the terminals. Devanahalli town and most plotted layouts sit further out. So you will still need a road trip to reach a station. Traffic on NH 44 near Hebbal can add a lot of time at peak hours.

Who Devanahalli suits, and who it does not

A good fit

  • Airport-belt workers who want a short drive once they build.
  • Patient buyers who can hold land for 7 to 10 years.
  • Families who want to design their own villa, not live in a tower.

A poor fit

  • Buyers who need rent from day one. An empty plot earns nothing.
  • Anyone who works in Electronic City, Sarjapur Road or Whitefield. The daily drive is long.
  • Traders who want a quick flip. Entry costs eat short-term gains.

Need a flat near the Outer Ring Road instead? See Adarsh projects in Bellandur or Adarsh projects in Panathur.

Checks before you buy a plot here

Most plot losses come from bad papers, not bad prices. Check the K-RERA record, the DC conversion order and the layout approval. Then check the khata type and a 30-year encumbrance certificate. Our checklist of 14 documents to check before buying a plot lists each paper. The A-khata vs B-khata vs E-khata guide explains why the khata type matters.

Plot loans work in a different way. Business Standard's plot loan guide says lenders may fund only 60% to 75% of a plot's value. Read our plot loan vs home loan guide and plot vs apartment in Bangalore before you apply.
