How the four projects differ

Adarsh Parkland Phase 2

Parkland Phase 2 has the biggest flats in the group. Its 3 BHK spans 1,850 sq ft, and the 2.5 BHK adds a study. It shares a campus of over 21 acres with Phase 1, per the Adarsh brochure. Adarsh lists Embassy Tech Village 2.7 km away. Read the Adarsh Parkland Phase 2 review.

Adarsh Rosewood

Rosewood is the only project here with a 2 BHK. The brochure lists 118 homes in a low-rise G + 4 format. Ecoworld sits 1.3 km away, per Adarsh. A small site means a shorter amenity list. Read the Adarsh Rosewood Bellandur review.

Adarsh Tropica Phase 2

Tropica Phase 2 has the smallest flats of the four, from 1,385 sq ft. Adarsh lists 30-plus amenities, including a tennis court and a pool. It has not published carpet areas, so read them on K-RERA. Read the Adarsh Tropica Phase 2 review.

Adarsh Welkin Park

Welkin Park is a township with Sky Residences towers of G + 25 floors, plus villas. Adarsh lists 75+ amenities and a 73,000 sq ft clubhouse. Its brochure puts TCS 4 km away. The Sarjapur Road area guide covers the wider belt.

What to check before you buy a flat

  • RERA number: Parkland, Tropica and Welkin Park each have more than one number. Make sure your agreement cites the one for your phase. Check it on the K-RERA portal.
  • Completion date: the date filed with RERA is the legal deadline. The brochure date is not.
  • Carpet area: compare it with the super built-up size. At Parkland, carpet is about 64–66% of the super area.
  • Title, khata and EC: ask for the title deed, the khata and the encumbrance record. The A khata vs B khata vs E khata guide explains why the khata type matters for loans and resale.
  • Taxes: add 5% GST on an under-construction flat, per PIB. Add 5% stamp duty, a 2% registration fee and about 0.6% cess, per Deccan Herald. That is about ₹12.6 lakh per ₹1 Cr.
  • Loan: Adarsh has not published approved banks for these projects. RBI caps home loans at 90% up to ₹30 lakh, 80% up to ₹75 lakh and 75% above. See the plot loan vs home loan guide.
