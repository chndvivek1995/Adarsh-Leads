Adarsh Palm Retreat Mayberry at a glance

%s is a block of 2 and 3 BHK flats inside the Adarsh Palm Retreat campus on the Outer Ring Road (ORR) in Bellandur. It sits next to the Adarsh Palm Retreat villas, close to Intel and Honeywell, per the Adarsh brochure.

The brochure shows six blocks, A to F, that rise to 13 floors. Adarsh built it in two phases. Phase 1 covers the floors up to the 7th. Phase 2 covers the 7th to the 12th floor. The homes on sale now are upper-floor flats, per Adarsh's current sales listing.

Adarsh's sales listing for October 2026 gives 312 homes in all, with 2 BHK flats from ₹2.07 Cr* and 3 BHK flats from ₹2.99 Cr*. It says the homes are nearing completion. Get both the price and the stage in writing before you book.

The project suits you if:

  • You work on the ORR between Marathahalli and Sarjapur Road, or at Wipro's Sarjapur Road office.
  • You want a flat that is close to ready, not one that is years away.
  • You want to live in a large, settled Adarsh campus.

It does not suit you if you need a home under ₹2 crore. Compare other Adarsh flats in Bangalore first. For a low-rise option a short drive away, see Adarsh Rosewood.

Adarsh Mayberry price

Adarsh's current sales listing starts 2 BHK flats at ₹2.07 Cr* and 3 BHK flats at ₹2.99 Cr*. These are indicative starting prices. Taxes, charges and the exact unit change the final figure.

The last column adds about 7.6% for stamp duty, cess and the registration fee in Karnataka, per Deccan Herald. It leaves out GST, parking, maintenance deposits and any floor-rise or view charges. Ask for a full cost sheet.

GST on a nearly ready flat

A flat bought before the completion certificate carries 5% GST, per the GST Council. A flat bought after the completion certificate carries none. Ask Adarsh which applies to your unit, and on what date.

RERA and legal

The brochure prints the Phase 1 RERA number as PR/KN/171110/002367. The upper-floor homes now on sale are in Phase 2, and the brochure does not print a Phase 2 number. Ask Adarsh for it and check it on the Karnataka RERA portal.

Check these items on the portal:

  1. The RERA number that covers your exact flat
  2. The completion date filed with RERA
  3. The occupancy certificate, if the block is ready
  4. Any complaints against the project
