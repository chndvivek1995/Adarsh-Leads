Adarsh Greens Phase 3 at a glance

Adarsh Greens is an Adarsh Developers campus near Jakkur Lake in North Bengaluru. The Adarsh website says over 70% of the land is open space, with a landscape inspired by forests.

Phases I and II are already registered on K-RERA. Adarsh's November 2026 sales sheet lists Phase 3 on 26 acres, with about 400 homes in 2 and 3 BHK sizes. It does not give a completion date yet.

Phase 3 prices and floor plans are not published. Ask the Adarsh sales desk for the Phase 3 price sheet and plans, and for its RERA number once it is filed.

The project suits you if:

  • You work in North Bengaluru, Hebbal or the airport belt.
  • You want a large, green campus with a full clubhouse.
  • You are happy to book early in a new phase.

It does not suit you if you need to move in soon, as Phase 3 has no completion date yet. Compare other Adarsh flats in Bangalore or see all Adarsh projects.

RERA and legal

The Adarsh website lists RERA numbers for Phases I and II only. Do not pay a booking amount for Phase 3 until you can see its own RERA number on the Karnataka RERA portal.

Check these on the Karnataka RERA portal:

  1. The RERA number that covers your flat
  2. The completion date filed with RERA
  3. The approved plan and carpet area
  4. Any complaints
