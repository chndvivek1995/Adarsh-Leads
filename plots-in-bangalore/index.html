What to check before you buy a plot

Run these checks on any layout, Savana included.

Approvals and RERA

  • Land conversion order: proves the land is cleared for non-farm use.
  • Layout approval: near the airport, BIAAPA (Bangalore International Airport Area Planning Authority) approves layouts. Ask for the release letter that covers your plot.
  • K-RERA record: match the project name, survey numbers, plot count and completion date on the K-RERA portal.

Adarsh Developers states that Savana has BIAAPA approval and A-khata. Ask for copies that name Phase 3. Adarsh lists only the Phase I RERA number, so find the Phase 3 number on the portal before you pay.

Title and khata

  • Title deed and mother deed: show that the seller owns the land and past owners were genuine.
  • Encumbrance certificate: shows no loans or claims. Get a fresh one just before you register.
  • Khata: an A khata means the property meets every legal and planning rule. A B khata signals a gap, and few banks lend on it.

Our 14-document plot checklist explains each paper. The A khata vs B khata vs E khata guide covers khata status in detail.

Loans

A plot loan pays for land only. A Business Standard guide puts plot funding at 60% to 75% of value, often for 5 to 15 years. A pure plot loan earns no tax break until the house is complete. Read plot loan vs home loan before you apply. Ask the sales desk which banks have approved the layout.
